About maillanka.com

maillanka.com is a small independent website that publishes original memes about work, technology and daily life, together with plain-English articles on email, online security, Wi-Fi troubleshooting and internet culture.

Who runs the site

The site is run by an IT professional who works with networks, servers and everyday office technology. That experience is where many of the jokes and the practical advice come from.

What we publish

We publish 16 original memes and 14 articles so far, and we add more regularly. Memes are created from scratch. Articles are written for people who want clear answers, and we avoid jargon where we can.

How the site is funded

The site is supported by advertising, including ads from Google AdSense. Ads help keep the content free for readers. Advertising never decides what we write. You can read how we handle cookies and advertising in our Privacy Policy.

Get in touch

Corrections, questions, copyright notices and meme ideas are welcome at info@maillanka.com. See also our editorial policy.
