About maillanka.com

maillanka.com is a small humor site. We make original memes about work, technology and everyday life, and we write short articles about why jokes work and how internet culture grew.

The site is run by an IT professional who spends the day fixing computers, networks and printers, which explains a lot of the jokes.

Our approach

Every meme is made from scratch, with original text and layouts. We do not copy other people's pictures or characters. If you see something that looks like it belongs to you, tell us and we will review it.

Advertising

The site is supported by advertising, including ads from Google. This keeps the content free for readers.
