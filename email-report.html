How we measure

We keep a list of well-known .lk and Sri Lankan organization domains across six sectors: government, universities and education, banking and finance, telecom and utilities, media, and business and retail. For each domain we look up four public records.

MX records say which servers receive the domain's email, which shows the provider, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or Zoho. If the MX points to an email security gateway, the real mailbox provider behind it cannot be seen. SPF lists which servers may send for the domain. DMARC says what to do with messages that fail checks, and it is enforced when the policy is quarantine or reject. DKIM is detected by checking common selector names, so a missing result can mean a custom name and not a missing record.

Limits: this is a sample of well-known domains and not every organization in Sri Lanka. It shows technical setup and not email volume, and nobody publishes how many emails are sent. Results are a snapshot of the scan date. To learn how to fix a weak result, read SPF, DKIM and DMARC explained.
