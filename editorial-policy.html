Editorial policy

Original work

Every meme on this site is created from scratch, with our own text and layouts. We do not copy other people's images, characters, logos or captions. Articles are written specifically for this site.

Accuracy

We aim to explain things correctly and in plain language. Where a topic changes quickly, such as settings in a product, we tell readers to check the provider's own pages for the latest details. We do not give legal, medical or financial advice.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it. Readers can report errors using the address on our Contact page.

Advertising

The site shows ads from Google AdSense. Advertisers do not influence what we publish, and we do not write sponsored posts disguised as editorial content. We never ask readers to click ads.

Respectful humor

Our jokes are about shared everyday situations. We do not publish content that targets people for who they are, and we do not publish hateful, violent, sexual or misleading content.

Privacy

We do not ask readers for personal information. See our Privacy Policy for how cookies and advertising work.
