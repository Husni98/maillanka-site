Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit maillanka.com and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal details directly. Like most websites, our hosting provider and our service providers may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the time of your visit.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, measure traffic and show advertising.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Other third-party vendors and ad networks may also place cookies. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where required by law, we show a consent message before cookies or similar technologies are used for personalized advertising or measurement. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy options link provided by the consent message.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Links to other sites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@maillanka.com.
